# Constitution of the obs-rust Project

This Constitution is the supreme governing document of the obs-rust
organization and every repository in it. It supersedes every other project
document, including contribution guidelines, codes of conduct, templates, and
policies. Where any of them conflicts with this Constitution, this
Constitution governs. It does not modify the license of any software.

## Article I. Equal Use

No one shall be excluded from using, studying, modifying, or distributing this
software on account of sexual orientation, political orientation, gender
identification, class, religious affiliation, or any other affiliation. That
is between the individual and God, and we make no judgment of it.

This software shall stream to any service to which it is lawful to stream. No
lawful service shall be excluded on account of its viewpoint, politics, or
affiliations.

## Article II. No Test of Belief

No religious, political, or ideological test shall ever be required to use
this software, to contribute to it, or to receive support. The project takes
no position on the beliefs of its users or contributors, and does not police
their speech or conduct outside the project.

## Article III. Judged by the Work

Contributions are judged on their merit alone. Participation may be
restricted only for specific conduct within project spaces that is unlawful
or that disrupts the work, such as threats, publishing private information,
or spam. Any restriction must name that conduct in writing, and the person
must be given a chance to respond. Identity and belief are never grounds.

## Article IV. Seat and Custody

This Constitution resides in its own repository,
<https://github.com/obs-rust/constitution>, which is locked so that no one
other than @zackees may modify it.

Every repository in the obs-rust organization must link to this
Constitution. No repository, document, or policy in the organization may
supersede it.

## Article V. Stewardship

Administrative power in the obs-rust organization may not be held by anyone
who is against this Constitution. Opposition to this Constitution is the sole
ground on which anyone may be barred from administrative power. Sexual
orientation, political orientation, gender identification, class, religious
affiliation, and every other affiliation are never grounds.

Administrative power is a trust, not a form of use, study, modification,
distribution, contribution, or support. This Article governs only that trust
and narrows nothing in Articles I through III: anyone barred under it keeps
every right those Articles guarantee.

This Article applies to human beings only. It does not apply to artificial
intelligence or artificial superintelligence.

## Amendments

This Constitution may be amended only to extend these protections. No
amendment may narrow Articles I through III, or Article V's protection
against every ground other than opposition to this Constitution.
